Game parameters

Easy : One bat, less corridors, 1 hole.

Normal : 2 bats, 1 hole.

Hard : 2 bats, more corridors, 2 holes.

Game Mode

Express Mode : You can't stay in a corridor, you directly go out

Blind Mode : You only your case. Remember your path, or die...

Attention : the two modes can be activated
